## Finding

Run `run-20260927T215939992Z-8b032045` detected all six negative controls. It then aborted in `runShortCorrectness` before the six ordinary sequences completed.

The failure came from `reconcilePage`. Its browser callback read `before`, a Node-side local, without serializing it into `page.evaluate`. The browser raised a `ReferenceError`. The callback now accepts `before` as its argument, and the caller passes that value to Playwright.

The run also exposed a context-isolation problem. The online-abort control can leave a queued occurrence in its browser's IndexedDB. `runShortCorrectness` reused `pages[0]` and required exactly one queued occurrence. The runner now creates a separately enrolled device and browser context for each negative control. The two refusal scans and their seed stay together in their dedicated context. The two render-tampering subcases use different contexts. The six ordinary sequences each use an independent fresh context; the duplicate seed and refusal share their sequence context. The aborted control's queued row remains in its own context until normal stack cleanup.

`createDevicePage` creates the enrolled device, context, observer, and page, and adds the context to the existing cleanup list. The four baseline profile pages still use aliases A through D and retain their existing profile allocation.
