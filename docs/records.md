# Historical records and assets

Records establish their stated baseline only. Preserve original logs, screenshots, hashes,
receipts, and probes. They do not grant permission to rerun mutations or prove current behavior.
Use the board for current ticket status and [known limits](known-limits.md) for current boundaries.
