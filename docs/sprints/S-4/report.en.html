S-4 makes voided tickets impossible to refund or re-send at claim time, pins the bulk refund budget rule, and gives operators a recorded end state for provider-failed refunds and refused exchange switches. Ready for the owner close decision; no tag or deployment.

All twelve non-closure members are Done on main 00e60199: the five planned stories (TKT-527, TKT-526, TKT-521, TKT-525, TKT-498), the simplification (TKT-579) and six members the owner added during the sprint (TKT-596 dependency updates; TKT-598, TKT-594, TKT-595 after round 1; TKT-602 after round 2; TKT-607 after round 3).

Every receipt at the candidate passed: the gate, the hermetic smoke with production images, the smoke-stack PostgreSQL service tests the owner ruled as the required tier, the focused tests, a host-Chrome browser run, the security workflow and CI. Every High from the four final-review rounds and the member reviews was fixed; none was overridden. Round 4, the last under the cap, returned HUMAN_REVIEW in all four areas.

What the owner should weigh at close: the security council never ran (claude-mythos-5-2 unavailable, no substitute); the outside-transfer path holds no reservation, so the runbook narrows but cannot close the race with an abandonment; ADR-087 accepts that a dispatch admitted before an abandonment can still move money; IMPL2-4 (Stripe duplicate JSON members, TKT-604) is an open Medium and an owner choice at close; DOC4-1 and IMPL4-1 (an impossible example in ADR-087 §14 and the same premise in the CLI refusal text, TKT-608) are deferred. The resend guarantee is at claim time, narrower than the goal's wording.
