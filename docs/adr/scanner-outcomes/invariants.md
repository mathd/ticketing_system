### 4. Invariants and the findings they answer

These are design requirements. TKT-558 delivers their untimed Entry/storage subset. Section 3.1 records TKT-559's Entry time delivery. TKT-399 delivers the Exit branches. Each has a test tier and a mutation in section 6.

- **I1. One final local outcome.** Before a final write, the page commits its decision to its coordinator. Only an in-time queue decision with verified producer eligibility makes a new live-page row reconcilable. ADR-085 D31 of TKT-556 preserves already-QUEUED pre-upgrade rows even when their old producer fails this test, M02. Rejection, definitive live 401, unconfirmed status/body, unclassified local exception, stopped scan, cancellation, and failed actuation are terminal, even if every terminal write fails. Exceptions cannot change their meaning. Only actual page loss admits the explicit unknown-PENDING recovery exception in I8. For normal live-attempt fallback, only a verified producer that received no Access response, or an Access result proved to precede every decision in this HTTP request, can reach the queue path under the Compose-only premise. P13 retains occurrence U; no per-occurrence proof is claimed. Section 2.4 enumerates these producers; the ADR-085 D24 of TKT-556 status set alone is not proof. Sent Exit uncertainty uses "Exit result unknown; the holder may leave", ADR-085 D36 of TKT-556. X01 is terminal, never offline. Refusal and actual page-loss recovery are separate transitions. Answers P5-1, P5-2, P5-3, P7-1, A7-1, DR1-1/2, and DA1-1. Delivery: ticket 2, with Access dependency 0 and time behavior in 3; Exit in 4. **Partial delivery:** TKT-558 implements the untimed Entry and storage subset. TKT-399 delivers the Exit branches; section 3.1 records TKT-559's Entry time delivery.
- **I2. Ownership follows the page.** Register the id before mint starts. Keep one page-owner lock through pending work, all decisions, failed writes, connection closure, replacement, version change, and component remount. The lease never defeats a held lock. No recovery path skips that lock. Answers A6-1, P5-1, P7-1, and A7-1. This also removes the per-outcome lock-release proof that failed in TKT-399. Delivery: ticket 2. **Partial delivery:** TKT-558 implements the untimed Entry and storage subset. TKT-399 delivers the Exit branches; section 3.1 records TKT-559's Entry time delivery.
- **I3. Persistence precedes its external effect.** A complete mint transaction precedes live send. A complete own-row, never-actuated mark precedes the single accepted opening instruction. Re-check time and generation after the mark. A false compare-and-set or a missing row cannot authorize opening. Queue instructions use the separate I1 in-memory decision before queue persistence; they do not claim that the queue write already committed. Answers F4, P5-2, and duplicate cases. Delivery: ticket 2 for persistence, 3 for time; Exit in 4. **Partial delivery:** TKT-558 implements the untimed Entry and storage subset. TKT-399 delivers the Exit branches; section 3.1 records TKT-559's Entry time delivery.
- **I4. Elapsed time governs every admission decision.** The 3000/30000 ms checks start at submission/detection and cover storage open, owner acquisition, recovery, mint, network, body decode, mark, and offline decision. A delayed timer or promise cannot bypass them. Use both production clock readings; fail closed on invalid or disagreeing readings. A delayed open never restarts an old presentation. Answers P2-1, A-1, P3-1/2, P4-1/2, and P5-2/3/4. Delivery: ticket 3; Exit in 4. **Partial delivery:** TKT-559 implements Entry checks and synchronous readiness refusal. TKT-399 delivers Exit behavior.
- **I5. Type and identity do not change.** Capture direction, credential, occurrence id/time, and pairing generation before waiting. All persistence and retries preserve them. Legacy absence means entry only under a known legacy format. Owner ADR-085 D13-3 of TKT-556 holds legacy PENDING rather than inferring an admission. Owner ADR-085 D31 of TKT-556 still syncs pre-upgrade QUEUED rows with indistinguishable causes; possible false facts remain, M02. New corruption never defaults to entry. Refusal is a separate entry-only variant. An old reader never sees a new-protocol queue. Answers F1, A-2, TKT-399 plan findings 5-7/12, and N4/5. Delivery: ticket 2 for Entry/storage barrier, 4 for Exit. **Partial delivery:** TKT-558 implements the untimed Entry and storage subset. TKT-399 delivers the Exit branches; section 3.1 records TKT-559's Entry time delivery.
- **I6. The result matches the local outcome and session.** No terminal outcome is described as queued or admitted. No failed write turns a known refusal into offline permission. Every Exit failure and duplicate says the holder may leave, without a denial-style alert. Where a request may have been sent and its result is unknown, say "Exit result unknown; the holder may leave". Say "Exit not recorded" only before send or after an Access refusal. A known accepted result still says it was recorded. A known local revocation always refuses Entry; a listed revoked Exit records nothing. Stop camera generations before resetting mode/pairing; clear prior results; a stale response cannot publish into a new session. Answers F2/3, P5-2/5, N2/3/6, and the Entry-reason limitation. Delivery: ticket 2 for Entry outcomes, 3 for time/session; Exit in 4. **Partial delivery:** TKT-558 implements the untimed Entry and storage subset. TKT-399 delivers the Exit branches; section 3.1 records TKT-559's Entry time delivery.
- **I7. Sync transports eligible rows only.** Sync has no actuation authority. It reads committed QUEUED rows; never repairs PENDING by guessing a live page's decision. Send exact captured identity and type. A malformed, foreign, duplicate, or incomplete response cannot retire any row. Authentication loss preserves already-eligible queue rows, including ADR-085 D31 of TKT-556 legacy QUEUED with unknown causes. Reconcile `conflict` means a duplicate_admit was recorded and alarmed, then becomes terminal locally. `rejected` is terminal without a successful recorded fact. Neither result instructs admission. ADR-085 D31 of TKT-556 permits pre-upgrade QUEUED rows with unknown causes, not only proved historical admissions. Answers F1, P7-1, A-2, N5/8, and the deferred sync-reporting finding. Delivery: ticket 2 for Entry, 4 for Exit. **Partial delivery:** TKT-558 implements the untimed Entry and storage subset. TKT-399 delivers the Exit branches; section 3.1 records TKT-559's Entry time delivery. **Sync-report delivery, TKT-551:** completed passes count recorded/synced, conflict and rejected separately. Rejected is not recorded and remains terminal RESOLVED. Earlier applied-batch totals stay beside a later failure until the next nonempty paired pass starts, even automatically and even if that retry applies nothing. A progress alert is first in the existing warning list and is removed at completion. Counts are per page and include matching results already retired by another page, not unique device-wide or physical-passage totals. Existing queue text can stay stale after authentication/storage failure until refresh; Last successful sync means a completed pass. The operator guide states the exact copy and transient-report limits.
- **I8. Page loss is named, not simulated by close.** Only absence of the page owner allows uncertain PENDING recovery, subject to lease/reload rules and the legacy hold in section 7.4. Preserve the refusal/admission tag and direction. A committed terminal or actuation mark never recovers. The page-loss recovery exception applies to new-format rows; owner-approved ADR-085 D13-3 of TKT-556 holds legacy PENDING instead. The old client does not preserve this lock boundary, E4/E5. ADR-085 D31 of TKT-556 separately accepts possible false facts, indistinguishable, from legacy QUEUED. The new-format unknown-PENDING exception can produce a signed false fact; do not call it physical evidence. Owner ADR-085 D13-4 of TKT-556 also requires O-W on the reloaded page for W01, with durable notice retention through sync retirement. Every compatible page renders all unacknowledged notices, irrespective of which page recovered or synced the row. Answers A6-1, P5-1, P7-1, DR1-4, and ADR-021's trust boundary. Delivery: ticket 2; Exit type in 4. **Partial delivery:** TKT-558 implements the untimed Entry and storage subset. TKT-399 delivers the Exit branches; section 3.1 records TKT-559's Entry time delivery.

TKT-558 D24 constrains I1: the queue classification path calls no storage API before its latch. If a later change adds an awaited or unawaited storage call there, reinstate the X01 storage-exception fixture at that production site before accepting the change. Clock injection belongs to ticket 3.

I3's two persistence orders are deliberate. For accepted live scans, the mark must precede opening. For an offline decision, the queue decision and instruction must precede an outcome write that could hang until after the deadline. The already-committed PENDING row plus the live owner lock bridge that second write. If the page dies in that gap, I8 states the remaining uncertainty. Moving the queue UI after the write recreates P5-2; minting directly QUEUED recreates P5-3.
